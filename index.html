Moto Mayhem · Game by Zaib

How to Play

Goal: race 7 rival bikers to the finish. Place top 3 to unlock the next track. Win cash, buy faster bikes and upgrades.

Steer: hold the left or right side of the screen (Touch), slide your thumb (Drag), or tilt your phone (Tilt, in Settings). Desktop: ← → arrows.
Throttle is automatic. BRAKE button / ↓ slows you down.

Fight: ride alongside a rival and hit ◀ PUNCH / KICK ▶ toward their side. Kicks reach further and hit harder but are slower. Desktop: A/D punch, Q/E kick.
Empty a rival's health bar to knock them off their bike for bonus cash. Grab a club for heavy hits. Knock out a rival carrying a club to steal it.

Watch out: crashing into cars or roadside objects damages your BIKE. If it hits zero you're wrecked. If your RIDER health runs out you get knocked down and lose time.
From Red Canyon on, a police biker patrols. Get knocked down near him and you're BUSTED (fine!). Or knock him out for a big bonus.

Pickups: $ cash · wrench (bike repair) · medkit (health) · blue nitro (speed boost) · club.
